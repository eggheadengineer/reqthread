# Heartbeat supervision timeout

The receiver shall de-energise the machine-enable output within 300 ms of the last valid heartbeat frame.

## Rationale

300 ms tolerates two consecutive lost frames at a 10 Hz heartbeat while staying inside the machine's stopping-distance budget.
